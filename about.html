About Heavens Party Treats

Heaven Party Treat (HPT) was founded by two sisters who were inspired after discovering creative party décor concepts online and developing a passion for the craft. The business was established in the summer of 2021 to create high-quality, personalised products for special occasions.

During the early stages of the business, we explored various designs, techniques, and creative styles to refine our skills and continuously improve the quality of our work. We initially specialised in handcrafted party favours before expanding our product range to include bespoke party decorations and event styling services.

Establishing a small business presented several challenges, including developing practical skills, building a customer base, and maintaining consistent quality. Despite these challenges, the experience has been both rewarding and professionally fulfilling.

Running Heaven Party Treat has strengthened our communication, customer service, organisation, and problem-solving skills, while also enhancing our creativity, attention to detail, and ability to manage client expectations. The business has also taught us the importance of patience, teamwork, and collaboration. Working together as sisters has enabled us to build a strong partnership, navigate challenges effectively, and support one another in achieving our shared goals.
